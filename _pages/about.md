  I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at the [Delft Center for Systems and Control 
  (DCSC)](https://www.tudelft.nl/en/3me/about/departments/delft-center-for-systems-and-control), TU Delft, working under [Prof. Bart De 
  Schutter](https://www.tudelft.nl/staff/b.deschutter/) on the ERC Advanced Grant project CLariNet.

  My research develops provably safe controllers for autonomous systems that must operate reliably under real-world uncertainties. I focus
  on **control barrier functions (CBFs)** — including robust, adaptive, predictive, and discrete-time variants — with formal 
  constraint-satisfaction guarantees. My work bridges rigorous control theory with experimental validation: I have designed and 
  field-tested safety-critical controllers on highway-driving heavy-duty trucks in collaboration with Navistar, Inc. (TRL 5–6).

  I received my Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from the [University of Michigan](https://umich.edu) in 2024, advised by [Prof. Gábor 
  Orosz](https://orosz.engin.umich.edu). I hold an M.Sc. from Bilkent University (2017) and a B.Sc. from the Middle East Technical 
  University (2013).  

  I am currently seeking **tenure-track faculty positions**.
