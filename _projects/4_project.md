In collaboration with **Allison Transmission, Inc.**, this project developed an energy-critical control framework for vehicle platoons. Analogous to safety-critical control, we introduced the concept of **energy-critical control** using Control Barrier Functions to enforce upper bounds on energy consumption while adapting to environmental conditions such as road grade and traffic density.
